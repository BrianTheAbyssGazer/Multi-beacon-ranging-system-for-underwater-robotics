\section{echos}
Let $d$ be the distance to a flat surface, $v$ be the speed of sound, $\delta(t)$ be the transmitted signal, $\alpha$ be the absorption coeeficient. the impulse response of the echo channel is $I(t)=\dfrac{\delta(t-T)Ae^{-\alpha T}}{\sqrt{d}}, T=2d/v$. For an unknown environment, an upper boundary of the impulse response of an echo channel is the sum of echos from surfaces at all possible distances $E(t)=\int^{\infty}_0 \dfrac{\delta(t-T)Ae^{-\alpha T}}{\sqrt{vT/2}}\dfrac{v}{2} dT=A\sqrt{\dfrac{v}{2t}}e^{-\alpha t}$.
Let $d$ be the distance to another beacon. Then the received response from it is $R(t)=\dfrac{\delta(t-T)Aexp(-\alpha T)}{\sqrt{d}}, T=d/v$.
